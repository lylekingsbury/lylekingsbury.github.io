About
I am a neuroscientist studying how the brain supports flexible decisions. As a Helen Hay Whitney postdoctoral fellow in Naoshige Uchida's lab at Harvard, I study how integrator dynamics in neural circuits and dopaminergic reward signals shape foraging decisions. During my Ph.D. with Weizhe Hong at UCLA, I studied how the cortex represents social information and how neural activity becomes correlated across the brains of socially interacting animals.
